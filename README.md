# Lonelytics
This application is a research and study project of Tampere University. It has been developed to explore the relationship between loneliness and mobile phone use. 
The app helps you track your loneliness scores with a short daily loneliness questionnaire, and your phone use through tables and charts. 
It also shows simple correlations between these data. The app is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses or treatment recommendations. 
If you have been feeling unwell or lonely for a long time, please contact healthcare services.

## Features
- **Streak counter** - keeps track of consecutive daily survey answers
- **Correlation analysis** - shows relationships between loneliness and other measurements
- **Daily survey** - short questions about current feelings of loneliness
- **Loneliness level chart** - visualizes trends in loneliness level over time
- **Phone usage tracking (day/night)** - displays how long phone has been used during day and night
- **Step counter** - records the number of steps taken during the day
- **Communication app usage** - shows total time spent in communication apps
- **Data export and reset** - allows data to be exported as a CSV file or deleted entirely

## Data collection
The app stores the following information on a daily basis:

- Your answers to the loneliness questionnaire and the resulting score.
- Your phone usage time, separated into daytime and night-time.
- Your daily step count.
- Your use of certain messaging apps (only total time, not message content).

All data is stored only on this device. The app does not send your data anywhere.
You can export or share your data as a CSV file from the app settings.
